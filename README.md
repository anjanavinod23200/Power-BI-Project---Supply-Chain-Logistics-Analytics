POWER BI PROJECT 

TITLE : Supply Chain Logistics Analytics 

DATASET DESCRIPTION :

        This Project analyses a Supply chain Dataset using Power BI. Supply chain dataset containing 100 SKUs across : Skincare, Haircare, Cosmetics.  It includes key fields such as Revenue, Profit, Products sold, Price, Inventory, Availability, Lead Time, Order Quantity and Customer Demographics. It used to analyze product performance, inventory status, supply chain efficiency and customer insights.

BUSINESS QUESTIONS :

1.	 What is the total revenue and product sales performance ?
2.	Which product types contribute the most to revenue and profit ?
3.	How does average revenue compare with the target ?
4.	How healthy is the current inventory position ?
5.	Which Product categories require the highest replenishment priority ?
6.	How efficient is the supply chain in terms of Lead Time ?
7.	How do Customer Demographics and Product portfolio vary ?

DASHBOARD :

The dashboard provides a visual summary of supply chain performance using interactive features like KPI, Cards, Slicers, Tooltips, Buttons, Bookmarks, Drillthrough and Conditional Formatting.

KEY FINDINGS :

●	Skincare is the strongest category in terms of revenue, profit and products sold.

●	Overall revenue is approximately 9% below the target.

●	27% of SKUs are low stock, with Skincare having the highest low-stock proportion (32.5%).

●	Haircare has the lowest average availability, requiring attention.

●	55% of SKUs have Medium Lead Time, making it the main supply-chain focus.

●	2% of SKUs have Long Lead Time and require investigation.

●	Skincare has the largest product portfolio with 40 SKUs.

●	Some SKU-level records show potential inconsistencies between Price, Products Sold and Revenue, which should be validated.


RECOMMENDATIONS :

●	Prioritize high-performing products, especially Skincare

●	Focus on closing the approximately 9% revenue gap.

●	Prioritize replenishment of low-stock Skincare products.

●	Investigate the lower availability of Haircare products.

●	Monitor Medium Lead Time products and investigate the Long Lead Time SKUs.

●	Use customer and product insights to improve product and pricing strategies

●	Validate potential SKU-level data anomalies before making detailed business decisions.

●	Regularly monitor inventory, availability, and lead-time performance.
